Station C

Grammatical range & accuracy · kill the band-6 errors

Grammar drills

Band 7 grammar means "frequent error-free sentences". These 36 questions target the errors that appear constantly in Thai speakers' writing: articles, subject–verb agreement, tense with time markers, prepositions after common academic words, and complex-sentence machinery. Answer honestly at speed — the category scoreboard shows where your points are leaking.

Station D

Task achievement · the overview is worth more than any number

Overview trainer

A clear overview is the difference between band 5 and band 7 Task Achievement — it's the first thing examiners look for. For each data scenario, write ONE or TWO sentences capturing the main trends without any specific figures. Then reveal the model and compare: did you synthesise (group the trends), use trend language, and avoid numbers?

Station E

Listening · dictation builds decoding + spelling under pressure

Dictation drill

IELTS Listening loses marks two ways: mishearing connected speech, and misspelling what you heard. Dictation trains both. Press play (British voice), listen ONCE at normal speed, type exactly what you heard, then mark. Use "slow" only to review after marking. Section 4 of the real test is a monologue full of sentences like these.

If nothing plays, your browser has no speech voices — try Edge or Chrome.

Band-7 functional phrases — steal these

Part 3 rewards functions: speculating, conceding, comparing. One natural phrase per function beats five memorised idioms. Practise dropping one of each into your timed answers.

Giving a measured opinion

  • As far as I can see, the main issue is…
  • I'd say it largely depends on whether…
  • My gut feeling is that… although I can see the other side.

Speculating about the future

  • I suspect we'll see more of this over the next decade.
  • It wouldn't surprise me if cities started to…
  • It's hard to say, but if I had to guess, I'd say…

Conceding before countering (band-7 gold)

  • That's true up to a point, but…
  • I take the point that… , having said that, …
  • Admittedly … , but on balance I still think…

Comparing & contrasting

  • Whereas my parents' generation…, people my age tend to…
  • It's nowhere near as … as it used to be.
  • There's a world of difference between … and …

Buying thinking time (without "umm")

  • That's a good question — I've never really thought about it, but…
  • Off the top of my head, I'd say…
  • Let me think… I suppose the honest answer is…
Station G

Fluency & rhythm · listen, then echo almost instantly

Shadowing

Shadowing means repeating a native speaker as they speak, or a beat behind — matching their rhythm and stress rather than translating in your head first. Press play, then record yourself saying the same sentence; your browser transcribes what it heard and shows how closely it matched, word for word. This builds the fluent, natural rhythm examiners register before they even judge your grammar.

Needs microphone access and a browser with speech recognition (Chrome or Edge, desktop or Android). Nothing is uploaded — recognition happens in your browser only.
